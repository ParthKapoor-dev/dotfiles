# Orbs: credits and licences

The shader orbs in this folder are ported from **Orbkit** by zzzzshawn
(<https://orbkit.zzzzshawn.cloud>, <https://github.com/zzzzshawn/orbkit>) to Qt Quick
`ShaderEffect` shaders for nox. `shdr-NN.frag`, `shdr-NN.frag.qsb` and `shdr-NN.json`
are generated by `nox/.local/lib/nox/orbs-build` from an Orbkit clone; `Orb.qml` is a
QML port of Orbkit's animation runtime (`orbs/core/orbkit-core.tsx`, MIT).

## Original Orbkit orbs (MIT)

shdr-11, shdr-12, shdr-13, shdr-14, shdr-16, shdr-17, shdr-21, shdr-23, shdr-24,
shdr-27, shdr-29, shdr-30, shdr-32, shdr-33, plus the shared prelude and runtime.
